How I work

  1. Get feedback early from clients, stakeholders and the teams closest to customers. The best ideas are the ones that survive real feedback.
  2. Iterate fast in Figma and share early. A rough idea people have seen beats a polished one nobody has.
  3. A design system is a promise of consistency, to users and to the developers building with it.
  4. Teams do their best work when they feel supported. Morale is part of the design process.
  5. Simplify the core tasks first. Everything else can wait.
